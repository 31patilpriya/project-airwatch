HARDWARE REFERENCE

Sensor guide

What each sensor measures, how to interpret its readings and important limitations.

M1
GAS SENSOR

MQ-135

Broad gas-response sensor commonly used in educational air-quality projects. Its output changes in response to several gases and environmental conditions.

Website unitRaw ADC (0–4095)
Not a direct CO₂ measurement or certified AQI sensor. Requires warm-up and calibration.
M2
GAS / SMOKE SENSOR

MQ-2

Metal-oxide sensor responsive to smoke and various combustible gases. The raw analog value is useful for observing changes in the sensor response.

Website unitRaw ADC (0–4095)
Not a calibrated gas concentration or life-safety alarm. Requires warm-up and calibration.
B
ENVIRONMENTAL SENSOR

BME280

Digital environmental sensor providing ambient temperature, relative humidity and barometric pressure over I²C.

Website units°C · % RH · hPa
Placement, heat from nearby electronics and airflow can affect measurements.
⚡

Electrical safety: ESP32 GPIO pins are not 5V tolerant. Typical MQ modules use a 5V heater supply; their analog output must be conditioned so it never exceeds 3.3V at the ESP32 ADC input. Use a shared ground with the external supply.
